## Overview

Survax is a machine learning project designed to predict childhood vaccination uptake using demographic, maternal, and healthcare access information. By identifying children who may be at risk of missing routine immunizations, the system aims to support more targeted public health outreach and improve vaccination coverage.

The project uses immunization records and household survey data from Narok County, Kenya, and explores how predictive analytics can strengthen decision-making in resource-constrained healthcare systems.

---

## Motivation

Vaccination is one of the most effective public health interventions, yet many children still fail to complete their recommended immunization schedules. Factors such as access to healthcare, maternal education, household characteristics, and prenatal care all influence vaccination outcomes.

Survax seeks to better understand these factors and provide a data-driven approach for identifying children who may benefit from early intervention.

---

## Project Objectives

- Predict completion of routine childhood vaccinations
- Identify the most influential predictors of vaccine uptake
- Compare multiple machine learning models
- Develop a reproducible workflow for public health prediction tasks

---

## Methodology

The modeling pipeline consists of:

- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Feature engineering from demographic and maternal health variables
- Model training and hyperparameter tuning
- Cross-validation and evaluation on held-out data
- Model interpretation using feature importance analyses

The primary models evaluated include:

- Logistic Regression
- XGBoost

Performance is assessed using classification metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, F1 score, and ROC-AUC.

## Impact

The long-term vision of Survax is to provide healthcare organizations with tools that help prioritize outreach to children at greater risk of incomplete immunization schedules. By supporting earlier intervention, predictive models like Survax have the potential to improve vaccination coverage while making more efficient use of limited public health resources.

---

## Future Development

Planned improvements include:

- Expanding support to additional vaccines and age groups
- Improving model interpretability for healthcare professionals
- Developing interactive dashboards for visualization and monitoring
- Exploring deployment within digital health workflows

---

## Takeaways

Survax demonstrates how machine learning can be applied to real-world public health challenges by transforming routinely collected healthcare data into actionable insights. The project combines predictive modeling with epidemiological principles to support evidence-based vaccination programs and improve child health outcomes.
